A local courier company built on pharmacy routes

Blue Route Logistics is a locally operated Florida courier company based in Port St. Lucie. We started with pharmacy deliveries, and that work still shapes how we operate: on time, accounted for, and easy to reach.

Who we are

Blue Route Logistics has direct, hands-on experience running scheduled pharmacy delivery routes on the Treasure Coast. We understand what a pharmacy needs from a courier: predictable pickup times, careful handling, confirmed hand-offs and a clear answer when something changes during the day.

We're now expanding our operations and taking on additional commercial delivery contracts with pharmacies, healthcare providers and medical businesses in Port St. Lucie, Fort Pierce, Stuart and Jensen Beach.

Because we're locally managed, you work directly with the people responsible for your deliveries. We keep our service area focused so we can stay dependable within it.

Our mission

To provide dependable, efficient and professional courier solutions that help healthcare businesses serve their communities with confidence.

Our vision

To become a trusted local logistics partner for pharmacies and healthcare providers throughout Florida's Treasure Coast.

How we work

The standards we hold ourselves to on every route.

Knowledge of local routes

We plan around the Treasure Coast's real roads, bridges, traffic and facility access, not a generic routing estimate.

Attention to delivery accuracy

Correct package, correct recipient, correct time, with confirmation that the delivery was completed.

Professional communication

Clear, courteous updates for your staff and the people receiving deliveries.

Commitment to dependable service

We agree on schedules and procedures up front and run to them consistently.

Long-term relationships

We're looking for partners, not one-off jobs. Recurring routes let us learn your operation and serve it better.

Continuous improvement

We review how routes perform and refine timing, sequencing and procedures as your needs change.

Why Blue Route Logistics?

A locally managed courier gives your pharmacy something a national network often can't: someone who knows your routes and answers when you call.
